# Example call brief (FICTIONAL)

This example is made up: Pete Hollis and Hollis Heating do not exist. It goes with the fictional profile of Sam Carter in [profile-example.en.md](profile-example.en.md).

How to use it: open the settings, paste the text below the line into the brief field and change it for your call. A brief is for this one call. Keep it short; the coach reads it again for every tip.

---

## Who
Pete Hollis, owner and founder. Trained as an engineer and still goes out to customers himself. His wife does the books and will probably not join the call.

## Company
Hollis Heating (fictional). 9 people. Boilers, air conditioning and underfloor heating. 4.6 stars on Google from 38 reviews.

## Goal of this call
First 30-minute intro call, after he replied to my email last week ("sounds interesting, give me a call"). Goal: a date for the free website check or a kickoff call. Do not mention a price unless he asks.

## What I already know
- His site is from around 2018 and does not work well on a phone. The phone number cannot be tapped on a mobile.
- There is no quote form. Requests come in through a general contact form and email.
- His Google Business Profile is well kept: recent photos and replies to reviews.
- One competitor in his area has a new site with a breakdown form.
- In his email he wrote that he "had a web company before that never finished". Ask about that calmly.

## What I want to learn
- How many quote requests come in through the site each month, and how many through other routes?
- Who built and runs the site now, and what does he pay for it each month?
- What went wrong with the previous web company?
- What is an average job worth to him, and what would one extra job a month mean?
- Who else decides (wife, business partner), and when does he want something live?

## Watch out
- He is busy and likes to talk about the technical side. Keep it short and steer with questions.
- Do not bring up Plus or the Care Plan before I know what he has now.
- End with a date for the check or the kickoff call.
